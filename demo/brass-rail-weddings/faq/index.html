Booking & payments

Do I need to schedule a tour, or can I just stop by?

Tours are by appointment only. Book online or call us at (413) 569-9585.

How do I reserve my date?

Dates are held first come, first served with a $500 non-refundable deposit. Start your booking here.

When are payments due?

A $500 non-refundable deposit is due when you sign your contract. The second deposit is due six months before your wedding, and the final payment three weeks before.

What forms of payment do you accept?

All major credit cards, debit cards, cash and checks.

Is there a guest minimum?

Our wedding minimum is 50 guests. Micro weddings in the Pub start at 20 guests.

How long is the rental?

Our packages include five hours. Additional hours are $250 per hour.

Spaces & ceremony

What is the capacity of each room?
  • Outside Deck: up to 250 guests
  • Main Ballroom: up to 200 guests
  • Lower Ballroom: up to 150 guests
  • Pub Room: up to 40 guests
  • Bridal Suite: up to 25 guests
  • Groomsmen Pub: up to 25 guests
Can we have our ceremony on site?

Yes. Choose the outdoor deck, a fireside ceremony in the Main Ballroom or our Lower Level ceremony space. Every room is available for your ceremony and cocktail hour. An additional rental fee applies when the ceremony is in a different space from your reception.

Is there a place for the couple to get ready?

Yes. The Bridal Suite has room for the whole bridal party, with a private bathroom, hair-and-makeup rooms, a full kitchen and a living room. The Groomsmen Pub offers a lounge, a changing room and a barber chair. See suite details and pricing.

Is the facility handicap accessible?

Yes. All of our banquet rooms and the outside deck are handicap accessible.

Is there valet parking?

Yes, valet parking is available for an additional fee. Your wedding manager can share the details.

Food & drink

Can we bring our own alcohol?

No outside alcohol is permitted. Our in-house bar offers a wide selection of beer, wine and liquor.

Can I bring my own cake or desserts?

Wedding cakes are welcome from licensed bakeries, with a $1.50 per person outside bakery service fee. Our in-house bakery can also handle it. See dessert options.

Do you accommodate food allergies?

Yes. Speak with your event manager for options and pricing.

Do you offer signature drinks?

Yes. Your event manager will help you design them.

Do you have children’s pricing?

Yes. Children’s pricing is offered once your menu is chosen, and we provide high chairs and booster seats.

Décor & setup

Can I bring my own decorations?

Yes. We just ask for no confetti, and nothing taped or adhered to the walls or fireplaces.

When can we start decorating?

Up to three hours before your event begins. Additional hours are $250 per hour.

Are linens included?

White or black floor-length tablecloths and napkins are included. Additional colors are available for an extra charge.

Do you provide centerpieces?

Centerpieces aren’t included, but you can drop them off before the wedding and we’ll set them up.

What size can our head table be?

Any size you like, or an intimate sweetheart table for two.

What size are the tables?

60-inch rounds that seat 6, 8 or 10 guests each. Square tables can be accommodated.

Can I bring table runners?

Yes. Please drop them off a few days ahead, opened and ironed.

Do you offer wedding planning services?

We don’t offer planning services, but we’re happy to recommend reputable planners. See our preferred vendors.

Still wondering?

The fastest answers come on a tour. Bring your questions and we’ll walk you through everything.
