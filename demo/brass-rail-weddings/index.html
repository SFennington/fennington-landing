Welcome

Inspiring, affordable and right on the lake.

The Brass Rail Meeting House is a lakeside wedding venue in Southwick, Massachusetts, with ceremony and reception spaces, private getting-ready suites and full-service catering under one roof.

Host forty of your closest people in the Pub or hundreds across the house. Our team handles the food, the bar and the setup so you can spend the day with the people you love.
