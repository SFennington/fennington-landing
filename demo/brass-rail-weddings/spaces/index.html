Event spaces

Room to celebrate.

Four ceremony and reception spaces and two private suites on one lakeside property, every one of them accessible.

At a glance

Up to 500 guests across the house.

Hold the ceremony in one space and the reception in another, or keep everything in a single room. Every space is available for ceremonies and cocktail hours.

Outdoors

Outdoor Deck & Gazebo

250guests

Say your vows with the lake behind you. The deck and gazebo host ceremonies, cocktail hours and the portraits you will frame.

  • Ceremony rental includes setup, chairs and an aisle runner
  • Lakeside backdrop for dusk portraits
  • Fully accessible

Indoors

Main Ballroom

200guests

The heart of the house. Gather everyone for dinner and dancing, then turn the stone fireplace into the backdrop for a fireside ceremony or your head table.

  • Fireside ceremony option
  • Any size head table or a sweetheart table for two
  • 60-inch rounds seating 6, 8 or 10

Indoors

Lower Ballroom

150guests

Our newest space. White columns, chandeliers and light floors make a classic canvas that suits any palette, for the ceremony, the reception or both.

  • Dedicated lower-level ceremony space
  • White or black floor-length linens included
  • Room for a full dance floor

Intimate

The Pub

40guests

Dark wood, candlelight and a fireplace glow. The Pub is made for micro weddings, with ceremony and reception for 20 to 40 guests.

  • Micro wedding packages from $50 per person
  • Cozy fireside setting
  • Full bar service

Getting ready

Two private suites, one easy morning.

Bridal Suite

Up to 25 guests · $1,000 + tax

A private bedroom, full bathroom, two hair-and-makeup rooms, a cozy living room, a full kitchen and a private entrance. Check in as early as 8 AM. Overnight stays are available.

  • Complimentary champagne and bottled water
  • Charcuterie board for the bridal party
  • Bar service available

For the groom’s side

Groomsmen Pub

Up to 25 guests · $500 + tax

A private dressing room and lounge with its own entrance, a barber-style chair and a 70-inch TV for the pre-game.

  • Complimentary champagne and bottled water
  • Charcuterie board
  • Bar service available
