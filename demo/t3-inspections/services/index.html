What’s inspected

Roof to basement, system by system.

Ted looks at the visible, accessible parts of every major system in the home and notes what’s working, what needs repair and what to keep an eye on.

  • Roof, gutters & chimney
  • Exterior, siding & grading
  • Foundation & structure
  • Basement & crawlspace
  • Electrical system
  • Plumbing & water heater
  • Heating system
  • Air conditioning
  • Attic, insulation & ventilation
  • Interior, windows & doors
  • Fireplaces
  • Garage & attached structures

Please make sure the utilities are on for the inspection. If they’re off, some systems can’t be tested and the inspection will be limited.

Add-on tests

Add them when you book, done in the same visit.

$175

Radon gas (air) test

A 48-hour test using a digital continuous radon monitor that takes a reading every hour. Results are ready as soon as the test ends, and the report is often emailed the same day.

Ted needs access to the home 72 hours before or 48 hours after the inspection to set up or pick up the monitor.

$65

Radon in water test

Radon can dissolve into groundwater and be released when you use water. Recommended for any home with a private well.
