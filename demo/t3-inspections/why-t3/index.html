Why T3 Inspections

Fast reports, easy booking, real know-how.

T3 Inspections is a local, owner-run business. Here’s what you get when Ted inspects your next home.

Book OnlineCall (413) 282-8616

Fast reports

Your home inspection report is guaranteed within 24 hours, and in most cases it’s emailed the same day. With the digital continuous radon monitor, the radon report is ready the same day the 48-hour test ends, too.

24/7 online scheduling

Schedule a home inspection right from your phone or computer. Click Book Online anywhere on the site to see exactly when I’m available.

I learned from experience how much it helps to know when your inspector is free. Whether you’re coordinating a time with your clients or working crazy hours, online booking is always ready. It makes buying or selling a home go a little smoother.

Knowledgeable

I can’t remember a time when I wasn’t learning about homes. Through a family business I was involved with building and construction from a very young age.

After college I got my real estate license and learned about homes from a completely different angle. As a realtor I saw how important a home inspector is to a real estate deal, and what makes a good one. In 2013 I started the extensive classes and training to become a home inspector, and two years later I opened T3 Inspections to serve this community.
