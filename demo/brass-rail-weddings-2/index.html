Your day here

How your day unfolds.

  1. Getting ready

    The bridal suite opens at 8 AM, with champagne and charcuterie waiting.

  2. The ceremony

    On the lakeside deck, under the gazebo or fireside in the Main Ballroom.

  3. Cocktail hour

    A charcuterie display is included, with passed bites if you'd like.

  4. Dinner

    Buffet or plated, from our own kitchen, starting at $67.95 per person.

  5. The party

    Five hours in the room of your choice, with a full bar.

    A sparkler exit to close the night.
