# Monthly Financial Tracker Review

Purpose: turn the financial tracker into a short monthly decision habit.

## Review Steps

1. Add missing receipts and purchases.
2. Group expenses by category.
3. Check whether recurring supply costs changed.
4. Note sales, farm income, or household offsets.
5. Decide one adjustment for next month.

## Questions

- Which category surprised you?
- Which supply should be purchased earlier next month?
- Which expense can be reduced without harming care quality?
- Did any sales or production offset costs?
- What is the one money decision for next month?
