The problem

Homestead chores fail when they live in memory.

Feed, water, egg collection, fence checks, garden watering, greenhouse work, bedding, compost, tools, supplies, and evening lockup can feel simple until the weather changes, someone is sick, or a normal routine gets interrupted.

This system gives your household one visible source of truth so daily work, missed chores, backup coverage, and exceptions are written down instead of guessed.

How the 14 days work

Build, test, simplify, and maintain

Days 1-4

Capture and organize

Inventory every recurring chore, sort by area and timing, separate frequencies, and build the master chore list.

Days 5-7

Assign and test

Create morning and evening checklists, assign owners and backups, then run the first full paper test.

Days 8-12

Track exceptions

Add notes, missed chore tracking, weekly anchors, animal/area sections, and failure-point review.

Days 13-14

Finalize the routine

Rewrite the clean system and set a short ongoing weekly review rhythm.

Actual product structure

Designed for the clipboard, not just the screen.

The worksheets include large writing areas, checkbox rows, owner fields, backup fields, and exception notes. The method is meant to be printed, marked up, reviewed, and replaced as your homestead changes.
