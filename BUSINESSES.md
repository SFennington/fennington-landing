## How to Create a New Business Site

### Quick Method:
Just say: **"Create a new [BUSINESS TYPE] site from the template"**

### Manual Method:
1. Copy `/templates/base/` folder
2. Rename to your business (e.g., `plumbing/`, `hvac/`, `roofing/`)
3. Edit `script.js` - update the CONFIG object
4. Optional: Edit `styles.css` to change brand colors
5. Done!
