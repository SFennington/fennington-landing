Privacy Policy for FireLife Discipleship

Effective Date

July 8, 2026

Overview

FireLife Discipleship ("the App") is provided by Fennington Apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and how to contact us with privacy questions.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 App Data You Provide

Devotional progress, reflections, prayer requests, repentance entries, onboarding responses, and theme preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

1.2 Account Information

No account is required for core use of the App. If optional account-based features are added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what information is collected and why.

1.3 External Links

The App may include links to external worship resources such as YouTube. When you open external links, those third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

Local app data is used to provide app functionality, including tracking devotional progress, saving reflections, maintaining prayer requests, recording repentance journal entries, and remembering settings.

3. Data Storage

Core FireLife data is stored locally on your device using device storage. If you uninstall the App or clear app data, local data may be deleted from your device.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your prayer requests, reflections, repentance entries, or devotional progress for advertising. We may disclose information only if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or with service providers necessary to operate future optional services.

5. Analytics

The current app code is designed around local storage for core app data. If analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, or account services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe those services and any related data handling.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Choices

You can delete locally stored app data by using any available in-app clear-data controls, clearing app storage through your device settings, or uninstalling the App.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updates are made, the effective date on this page will be revised.

10. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us:

Email: privacy@fennington.com

Website: https://fennington.com/firelife
