The problem

Most homesteads run on memory until something breaks.

Feeding, watering, egg collection, fence checks, garden beds, supply runs, vet numbers, and receipts all live in different places — sticky notes, a whiteboard, a phone, or nobody's head in particular.

This is not a beginner's guide to starting a homestead. It assumes you already have the animals, the garden, and the routines, and that you want them organized and written down instead of guessed at.

FAQ

Common questions

What file formats do I receive?

The 11 worksheets are print-ready PDFs, sized for US Letter. The ebook is a DOCX file that opens in Word or Google Docs. The complete kit also includes the editable Markdown and CSV originals of every worksheet, so you can change the columns and reprint.

Is this a beginner's guide to starting a homestead?

No. It assumes you already have your property, animals, and routines. The focus is organizing and recording what you are already doing.

Is the Livestock Tracker app required?

No. Every part of the system works on paper. The app is mentioned only as an optional way to keep records digitally.

Does this give veterinary or medical advice?

No. The emergency protocol and vet contact sheet is for recordkeeping and quick reference only. Contact a qualified veterinarian for any animal health decision.
