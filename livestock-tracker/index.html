Frequently Asked Questions

Privacy

Your Data Stays on Your Device

Livestock Tracker is designed with privacy first. All your farm data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your livestock records, financial data, or personal information on our servers.

No Account Required

You don't need to create an account or provide any personal information to use Livestock Tracker. Download the app and start using it immediately—no email, no password required.

Offline-First

The app works completely offline. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it for backup purposes.

Optional Backups

You control your backups. If you choose to backup your data, it's stored in a location you specify (like your personal cloud storage). We never have access to these backups.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect minimal, anonymized technical data to improve app reliability and performance (for example, feature usage statistics). This data contains no personal farm information and cannot be traced back to you or your farm. Analytics collection is active and cannot currently be opted out.
