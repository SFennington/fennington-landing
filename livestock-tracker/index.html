Frequently Asked Questions

Privacy Policy

Your Data Stays on Your Device

Livestock Tracker is designed with privacy first. All your farm data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your livestock records, financial data, or personal information on our servers.

No Account Required

You don't need to create an account or provide any personal information to use Livestock Tracker. Download the app and start using it immediately—no email, no password, no tracking.

Offline-First

The app works completely offline. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it for backup purposes.

Optional Backups

You control your backups. If you choose to backup your data, it's stored in a location you specify (like your personal cloud storage). We never have access to these backups.

Analytics

We collect minimal, anonymized usage analytics to improve the app (e.g., which features are most used, crash reports). This data contains no personal information and cannot be traced back to you or your farm.

If you have questions about privacy, contact us at privacy@fennington.com
