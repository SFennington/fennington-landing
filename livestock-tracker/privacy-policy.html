Privacy Policy for Fennington Livestock Tracker

Effective Date

March 19, 2026

Overview

Fennington Livestock Tracker ("the App") is provided by Fennington Apps. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how data is handled. This Privacy Policy applies to users worldwide and explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 App Data You Provide

Animal records, breeding notes, egg logs, health entries, financial data, and related farm information you enter are stored locally on your device. We do not sell this data and do not use it for advertising. This data remains under your control at all times.

1.2 Account and Personal Information

No account is required for core use of the App. If you choose to use optional account-based features (such as cloud backup or cross-device sync), we may collect limited personal information such as your email address and name. This information is used solely to provide those services.

1.3 Technical Data and Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect limited technical data to improve the App's reliability and performance. This includes:

  • App performance metrics
  • Feature usage statistics (aggregated and anonymized)
  • Device information (e.g., device model, operating system version)

This technical data is anonymous and aggregated. We do not collect personal farm records for analytics purposes. The data is processed by Google LLC in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 App Data

App data you enter is stored locally on your device and used solely to provide the App's functionality. We do not access, analyze, or use this data for any other purpose.

2.2 Technical Data and Analytics

We use technical data and analytics to:

  • Improve App stability and fix bugs
  • Understand which features are most used to guide development
  • Monitor App performance
  • Ensure the App works correctly on different devices

3. Legal Bases for Processing (EU Users)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we rely on the following legal bases for processing personal data:

  • Legitimate Interest: To improve App performance and stability (technical diagnostics and analytics)
  • Contractual Necessity: To provide account-based features you choose to use
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with applicable laws when required

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We only share data in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With third-party service providers (such as Google for Firebase services) who perform services on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, to protect our rights, or to comply with legal processes
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (subject to appropriate protections)

5. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Technical data and analytics may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. When we transfer data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

6. Data Retention

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you remove it. Technical data and analytics are retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. You can delete app data at any time by uninstalling the App or using in-app delete/export controls.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Portability: Request a copy of your personal information in a structured format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@fennington.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

9. Analytics Controls

Technical data collection through Firebase Analytics is enabled by default to help improve the App. Analytics collection is active and cannot currently be opted out.

If analytics controls are added in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be updated with exact steps.

10. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updates are made, we will revise the effective date on this page. For material changes, we will provide notice through the App or other appropriate means. Your continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@fennington.com

Website: https://fennington.com/livestock-tracker

13. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the United States. For users in the European Economic Area, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.
