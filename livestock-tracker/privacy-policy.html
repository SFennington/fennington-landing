Privacy Policy for Rork Livestock Tracker

Effective Date

February 16, 2026

Overview

Rork Livestock Tracker ("the App") is provided by Fennington Apps. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how data is handled.

Data You Enter in the App

Animal records, breeding notes, egg logs, health entries, financial data, and related farm information you enter are stored on your device. We do not sell this data and do not use it for advertising.

Account and Personal Information

No account is required for core use of the App. If optional account-based features are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated before those features launch.

Diagnostics and Analytics

We may collect limited technical diagnostics such as crash data and basic usage events to improve reliability and performance. This information is used to maintain and improve the App.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We only share data when required by law, to protect users, or with service providers strictly for operating and improving the App.

Data Retention

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you remove it. Diagnostic data retention depends on provider defaults and operational needs.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can remove app data by uninstalling the App and/or using in-app delete/export controls where available. You can contact us to request clarification regarding this policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updates are made, we will revise the effective date on this page.
